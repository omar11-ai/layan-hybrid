feat: LAYAN - the anti-scam persona agent, with hybrid inference via Firebase AI Logic

Most people block scam calls. Blocking ends the interaction and gives the
police nothing: no bank account, no mule name, no payment channel, nothing
for a police report or a bank's fraud desk.

LAYAN does the opposite. It keeps the scammer on the line. A scripted persona
- Puan Rina, 54, a retired school teacher from Kepala Batas, Penang - stalls,
asks naive questions, sounds slightly hard of hearing, and never hangs up.
Meanwhile an indicator extractor pulls the mule account, the phone number, the
account name, the payment channel, the amount demanded and the pressure tactic
out of every message.

The session ends in an evidence pack: the scam archetype fingerprint, the
full transcript, a deduplicated indicator table, the pressure-tactic timeline,
and the recommended next actions.

Why the split is hybrid
-----------------------

The reply path is on-device (Gemini Nano through Firebase AI Logic, falling
back to the cloud model, falling back to a deterministic local script),
because the persona must never stall when the network drops - which is
exactly when a scammer pushes hardest.

The evidence path is cloud-first (Firestore, Auth, App Check), because the
evidence has to be durable, auditable and shareable. If the phone dies
mid-engagement, the cloud still has the session and the indicator timeline.

The UI states honestly which path produced each reply: ON_DEVICE or CLOUD.

What is in here
---------------

- src/engine/ - the persona engine: archetype selection, the script state
  machine, turn taking, and the indicator extractor. Pure and deterministic.
- src/ai.ts - Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference, PREFER_ON_DEVICE with an
  inCloudParams fallback, and the inferenceSource the UI renders.
- src/firebase.ts - App Check, anonymous Auth, Firestore and Remote Config.
- src/cloud.ts - durable session sync.
- src/App.tsx - the operator dashboard: transcript, indicator timeline, the
  evidence pack.

The scammer side is a scripted simulator. It makes no telephony calls and
contacts no real numbers.
